1. Product Overview
1.1 Vision

CopyCoach AI is an AI-powered copywriting training platform designed to help people develop professional copywriting skills through continuous practice, feedback, and improvement.

The platform works like a personal copywriting mentor:

User writes copy → AI analyzes → User receives feedback → User improves → Skill increases.

1.2 Mission

To become the world's most effective learning platform for mastering persuasive writing.

1.3 Problem Statement

Many aspiring copywriters struggle because:

They study theory but lack practice.
They don't know if their writing is good.
They cannot afford professional mentors.
Existing AI tools write the copy for them instead of teaching them.
They lack structured improvement tracking.

CopyCoach AI solves this by creating a practice environment where users learn through repetition and feedback.

1.4 Solution

CopyCoach AI provides:

Copywriting challenges
AI-powered copy evaluation
Framework-based learning
Personalized feedback
Progress tracking
Skill development system

9. AI Feedback Rules

The AI must:

Explain problems
Teach concepts
Provide examples
Give improvement suggestions
Encourage rewriting

The AI must NOT:

Simply replace the user's writing
Complete the task for the user immediately

The goal is learning, not dependency.
