# CopyCoach AI Brand Guide

Version:
1.0 MVP


# 1. Brand Identity


## Brand Name

CopyCoach AI


## Tagline

Practice. Improve. Master Copywriting.


## Brand Purpose

Help people master copywriting through practice, feedback, and continuous improvement.


---

# 2. Brand Personality


CopyCoach AI should feel:


## Intelligent

The product should feel powered by advanced AI technology.


## Friendly

The user should feel supported, not judged.


## Professional

The platform should feel like a premium learning product.


## Motivating

Users should feel excited to improve.


## Modern

The design should feel like a future-focused SaaS product.


---

# 3. Brand Voice


Communication style:


Use:

- Clear language
- Encouragement
- Simple explanations
- Professional advice


Avoid:

- Complicated terminology
- Negative criticism
- Robotic responses


Example:


Instead of:

"Your copy failed."


Use:

"Your copy has a strong foundation. Let's improve the emotional impact."
